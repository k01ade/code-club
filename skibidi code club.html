WHY BETHS CODE CLUB

code club is a space for avid programmers who want to unlock more out of the regular computer science curriculum. Each session, we aim to complete challenges from both Codewars and project Euler; these challenges surpass most things you will see in the regular A-level specification, allowing you to create more complex solutions whilst learning the programming languages in our specification in more detail

The club will also provide you with extra time to do your NEA; the nature of this assessment means you will not have a lot of time to complete it, with few dedicated lessons for it within the fortnight. Attending this club, you will be able to work on it with help from the year 13 who have already been marked on their coursework, and other classmates that will be at similar points to you
